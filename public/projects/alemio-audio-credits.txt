Used under the book title, author and Part One at the start, and under each chapter heading, nowhere else. The original treatment excerpted, filtered, gently compressed, level-adjusted, faded and mixed the music under spoken drama. Its pitch and speed are unchanged, and the existing heading/title music samples are preserved in this edition.

There is no music under the story. ‘The Long Dark’, ‘Intervention’ and ‘A Kind Of Hope’ are not used.

The inherited wind, water and birds were filtered, looped where needed, held at one level with slow swells evened out, and mixed under the voices. In this edition only ambience within replacement speech intervals is fitted to new natural lengths. Each spot cue retains its complete source samples, level, duration and story anchor; effects are not repeated to fill a changed voice interval. Short sounds retain their existing excerpting, filtering and fades. The heart’s pulse is bell-derived resonance, not a recorded heartbeat. No source was used to train a model or clone a voice.
